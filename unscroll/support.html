Subscriptions

How do I cancel a subscription?

Pro subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, select Unscroll Pro and cancel. You can also go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. To stop renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore purchases on the paywall, or open Settings in Unscroll and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Locks

A lock does not start

  • Check Screen Time access in iOS Settings → Screen Time. Without Screen Time access, Unscroll cannot block anything.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode. Screen Time blocks can behave unreliably while it is on.
  • Restart your iPhone, open Unscroll again and check your lock profile.

How do I end a lock in an emergency?

Open iOS Settings → Screen Time and revoke Screen Time access for Unscroll. This ends any running lock.

Unscroll does not unlock an Iron lock inside the app before its window ends. Phone calls and emergency services are never blocked by Unscroll.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

Unscroll has no account and no server operated by us. Your data, including your first name, birth year, quiz answers, lock profiles, unlock history and streak, stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it. We hold no copy of it.

While an Iron lock runs, iOS prevents deleting apps. End the lock first, as described above.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For how data held by RevenueCat is handled, see the Privacy Policy.
