Unscroll blocks the apps you choose, shows your real screen time and what it adds up to over a lifetime.

Lock profiles

Block the apps you choose

Pick the apps to block and set the time windows in which they stay locked. Unscroll applies the blocks with Apple's Screen Time APIs.

Three levels

Breath, Firm or Iron

Breath gives a short pause, then 5 minutes of access, up to three times a day. Firm allows one emergency unlock per day after a 60-second wait and typing a sentence. Iron allows no unlock until the window ends; while an Iron profile is switched on, iOS also prevents deleting apps.

Screen time

Your real numbers

See your screen-time statistics and a life calculation: a simple projection of the lifetime you would spend on your phone, assuming a life expectancy of 80 years. It is not medical or scientific advice.
