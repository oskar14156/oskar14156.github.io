2. In short

Unscroll has no user account, no login and no server operated by us. Your personal settings and history stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the purchase flow needs: Apple processes payments, and RevenueCat checks your subscription status. There is no advertising, no tracking across apps, no analytics SDK and no selling of data.

3. Data stored only on your device

Unscroll stores the following locally on your device: your first name, your birth year, your answers to the onboarding quiz, your lock profiles, the history of your unlocks and your streak.

Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it.

4. Screen Time data

Unscroll uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to block apps and to show your statistics. Screen-time data, such as minutes, app names and pickups, is processed and stored only on your device. Detailed reports are drawn inside an Apple-sandboxed extension; the app additionally keeps a simple daily total of your phone time on your device for its own charts. None of this is sent anywhere. We never receive this data and cannot access it.

Unscroll does not know which apps you selected. It only holds opaque tokens provided by Apple.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

To validate and manage subscriptions, Unscroll uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID,
  • your purchase and subscription status,
  • basic device and app information, such as OS version, app version, country or locale, and your IP address, which is processed only transiently.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Apart from this purchase flow, nothing leaves your device.

6. Permissions

  • Screen Time access is needed to block apps and to show your statistics. Without it, Unscroll cannot block.
  • Notifications are used for the trial-ending reminder and the weekly report.

Both permissions are optional. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings. You can revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings → Screen Time at any time. This also ends any running lock.

7. What we do not do

Unscroll contains no advertising, no tracking across apps and no analytics SDK. We do not sell data.

8. Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device. Unscroll has no account to close.

The data held by RevenueCat is limited to the anonymous app user ID and your subscription status. To exercise your rights regarding this data, contact us at the address in section 12; we will help you with your request. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. See the Support page for how to cancel.

9. Children

Unscroll is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the EU where the GDPR requires parental consent unless national law sets a lower age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. GDPR information for users in the EU and EEA

Legal bases

We process purchase and subscription data to perform our contract with you and to provide subscriptions (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Because Unscroll does no tracking, we do not rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for any tracking.

International transfers

RevenueCat is located in the USA. According to RevenueCat's policy, transfers of personal data to the USA are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept as described in its privacy policy.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, write to oskarpolster.business@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde (Austrian Data Protection Authority), dsb.gv.at.
