FAQ

Does ActionMerge upload my clips?

No. All container inspection, visual/audio boundary fingerprinting, and remuxing run 100% offline on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does GoPro or DJI telemetry stay intact?

Lossless remux mode copies original video, audio, and metadata tracks (such as GoPro gpmd GPS/gyro, DJI djmd, and tmcd timecode) directly into a 64-bit MP4 without re-encoding.

Why was a clip flagged as ignored or incompatible?

Low-resolution proxy files (.LRV), thumbnails (.THM), byte-identical duplicates, and parallel dual-lens 360 streams are automatically separated so they never corrupt your main recording.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage or cancel a subscription in your Google Play or Apple App Store account. Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription.
