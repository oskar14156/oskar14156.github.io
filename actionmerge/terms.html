What ActionMerge does

ActionMerge lets you import split action camera, dashcam, and smartphone video chapters, analyze visual, audio, and metadata continuity offline, review overlap or missing-chapter seams, and export a continuous MP4/MOV file without re-encoding whenever formats match.
