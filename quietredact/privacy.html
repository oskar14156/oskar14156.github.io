Privacy Policy & Datenschutzerklärung

Effective 23 November 2026

QuietRedact is a privacy utility for iOS and Android. The documents you redact are processed entirely on your device. This policy describes what the app, its purchase provider, and its crash-reporting provider actually process. The controller is Oskar Polster, Grafenberg 116, 3730 Grafenberg, Austria — see Imprint.

Documents, text and faces — processed on your device only

When you import a PDF or image, QuietRedact analyses it locally on your device — using Apple's on-device Vision and PDFKit frameworks on iOS, and Android's PdfRenderer, PdfDocument, and on-device ML Kit models on Android. Text recognition, the detection of names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, IBANs, card numbers, document IDs and dates of birth, and face detection all run locally on your phone or tablet.

QuietRedact has no document server. Your documents, the recognised text, the detected values and the exported result are never uploaded to us, to RevenueCat, to our crash-reporting provider, or to any other third party. They are not used to train any model. There is no cloud OCR path in this app.

Files created while you work

To analyse, preview and export a document, the app creates temporary working copies in its own private sandbox (protected by iOS file protection on iOS and excluded from Google Auto Backup via allowBackup=false on Android). These are deleted when the session ends, when you leave the document, or on the next launch after a crash. QuietRedact only deletes files it created itself — your originals in Files, Google Drive, Photos or Gallery are never modified or removed.

Photos, Gallery and Files access

QuietRedact reads only the specific file or photo you choose through the system Document Picker (Storage Access Framework on Android) or system Photo Picker. It does not request broad media-library or storage permissions on Android and does not browse your files in the background. The camera and microphone are not used.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). QuietRedact uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to verify whether the Pro entitlement is active and to restore purchases. For that purpose RevenueCat processes purchase history, product identifiers, entitlement status, country/storefront, device and app version information, and an anonymous app user identifier generated on the device.

QuietRedact has no user account. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email address, phone number, Apple Account, Google account, or any document content. We cannot connect a purchase record to your identity.

Crash and error reports

To diagnose crashes and technical errors, we use Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA), with data stored in the EU (Frankfurt am Main); privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy. When the app crashes or encounters an unexpected error, a technical report is sent containing:

  • the error message and code location (stack trace),
  • technical metadata such as app version, operating system, device model, and UI language,
  • the last technical app steps (breadcrumbs) before the error, and basic performance metrics (app start and frame stability) sampled from 1 in 10 sessions.

Reports never contain documents, recognised text, filenames, storage paths, email addresses, IBANs, card numbers, user IDs, IP addresses, advertising IDs, screenshots, or session replays. The app strips paths, URIs, filenames, and sensitive patterns on-device before sending, and the server applies matching scrubbers and drops IP addresses. Reports are automatically deleted after at most 90 days. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in app stability and security).

No tracking, no advertising, no behavioural analytics

QuietRedact contains no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK, and no behavioural analytics SDK. It performs no tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA / AD_ID).

Retention

App settings and the entitlement cache stay on your device until you delete the app. Temporary document files are removed as described above. Crash reports are retained for at most 90 days. RevenueCat retains purchase records for as long as needed to keep purchases restorable. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request.

International transfers

RevenueCat, Inc. and Functional Software, Inc. are based in the United States (Sentry event data is hosted in the EU, Frankfurt am Main). Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, the transfer is based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses concluded with the provider.

Children

QuietRedact is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data from children under 13.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at. Because there is no account, most of your data never leaves your device: deleting the app removes it. For purchase-related requests write to oskarpolster.business@gmail.com. See Data deletion.
