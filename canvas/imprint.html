Imprint

Disclosure under §5 ECG and §25 MedienG.

This is a static preview build, not the operating site. Operator details are published on the live site, which is the one that actually takes payments.

Before launch, check three things: whether your activity needs a Gewerbeanmeldung (and add the GISA number and the issuing authority here if it does); whether you are still under the VAT threshold when you launch (if not, replace the VAT line with your ATU number); and whether you want your home address published — see below.

Purpose of this site

Operating a collaborative pixel canvas where users purchase pixel placements, with a published share of every payment allocated to a charity partner. Editorial responsibility under §25 MedienG lies with the operator named above.

Online dispute resolution

The European Commission provides a platform for online dispute resolution at ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr. We are not obliged and not willing to participate in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board.

Reporting illegal content

Reports under Article 16 of the Digital Services Act can be made through the report function on any pixel of the canvas, or by email to the address above. Every report is reviewed by a person, and you are told the outcome.
