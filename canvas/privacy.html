Privacy

What we collect, why, and how to get rid of it.

Before launch: add the controller name and address, confirm your processors, and have this reviewed. The technical description below matches what the software actually does.

The short version

No tracking pixels. No advertising networks. No analytics that follow you elsewhere. One cookie that identifies your artwork, and the payment data your card provider needs.

Images you import as a reference are never uploaded. They are decoded in your browser. Only the pixels you decide to claim are ever sent to us.

Card details are handled by the payment provider. We never see or store your card number.

Legal basis

Performing our contract with you (your purchase and its display), our legal obligations (accounting, illegal content), and our legitimate interest in a site that works and is not abused. We do not rely on consent for anything essential, which is why there is no cookie banner asking you to accept tracking — there is no tracking to accept.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Contact details are in the imprint.

One honest limit: if you ask us to delete your data, we remove your personal information — name, session, contact link — but the artwork itself stays on the canvas. It was bought by someone, it is part of a collective image other people also paid into, and it appears in snapshots that we promised never to edit. What we can do is disconnect it from you. If that is not acceptable to you, do not buy pixels.

Payment records cannot be deleted before their statutory retention period ends.

You can complain to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde).

Who else sees your data

Our hosting provider, our payment provider, and nobody else. We do not sell data, and we do not share it with advertisers, because there are none.
