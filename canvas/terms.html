Terms of use

Plain language first, because terms nobody reads protect nobody.

Before launch: have these reviewed by a lawyer and replace the operator details in the Imprint. This is a working draft written to be honest, not a substitute for legal advice.

1. What you are buying

You buy the right to place and display an image on specific pixel positions of a specific canvas, for the period you paid for. You are not buying ownership of part of a website, a share of the project, or any property right in the canvas itself.

Permanent means for the life of that canvas, including its printed version and its public archive. Rental means seven days from purchase, renewable.

You keep the copyright in whatever you draw.

2. What you grant us

By placing artwork you grant us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to display, reproduce and distribute it as part of the canvas — on the site, in snapshots and timelapses, in share images, in printed reproductions of the canvas, and in material promoting the project.

This licence covers the canvas as a whole. It does not let us license your artwork on its own to third parties, and it does not transfer your copyright.

3. What you may not place

Illegal content of any kind. Nazi, extremist or terrorist symbols and propaganda. Hate content. Sexual content, and absolutely any sexual content involving minors. Personal data about others, doxxing, credible threats. Scams and fraud. Content infringing someone else's copyright or trademark. Links to any of the above.

We remove prohibited content whether or not it was paid for. Buying pixels does not buy the right to publish illegal content.

If your artwork is removed for a policy breach you do not get a refund, but for a permanent placement the pixels stay yours and you may draw something permitted instead. Repeat or serious breaches end the account and the placement.

4. Moderation and reporting

Artwork is reviewed by a person before it appears. Anyone can report any pixel using the report function, without an account. Reports are reviewed by a person, and we will tell you the reason if we remove your artwork.

If you think a removal was wrong, reply to the notice and a person will look again.

5. Your right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the EU you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a purchase of digital content. Because artwork is placed as soon as it is approved, we ask you to expressly consent to immediate performance and acknowledge that you lose that right once it goes live. That is the checkbox at checkout — it is not decoration.

If you do not tick it, we cannot place your artwork.

If we fail to deliver — the artwork never appears, or we remove it for a reason that is our fault — you get your money back.

6. The charity share

A published percentage of every completed, non-refunded payment is allocated to the charity partner, calculated on the gross amount before any of our costs. The current percentage and the full breakdown of the rest are on the money page, which is part of these terms.

This is a purchase, not a donation. It is not tax-deductible for you. We do not claim otherwise anywhere, and if you ever find us doing so, that is a mistake we want reported.

If a payment is refunded or charged back, the corresponding charity allocation is reversed.

7. The canvas itself

The rules in the Canvas Constitution are binding on us: fixed size, never resized, price fixed while the canvas is open, sealed at the end, snapshots never edited, and new rules never retroactively reducing placement rights that were already bought.

If the project shuts down, the final canvas is published in full resolution and archived publicly.

8. Liability

We run this as carefully as one person can, but we do not promise uninterrupted availability. Our liability is limited to the amount you paid, except where the law does not allow that limit — in particular for personal injury, intent, and gross negligence. Your statutory consumer rights are unaffected.

9. Changes

We may change these terms for future purchases. Changes never reduce rights in placements you already bought. Material changes are announced on the site before they take effect.
