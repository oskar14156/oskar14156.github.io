Privacy choices

You choose what leaves your device.

The app has no advertising or cross-app tracking and does not upload source videos to our servers.

Media sharing

Video processing is on-device. The app sends an export outside the app only when you explicitly choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Cancel the share sheet to keep the export on your device.

Local data controls

Use Settings → Clear generated cache to remove locally generated files and history. This does not remove the original source video. See Data deletion for full steps.

Purchases

If you make or restore a purchase, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information to provide the entitlement. This is separate from video processing. Apple Account settings let you manage or cancel subscriptions. Contact us at oskarpolster.business@gmail.com for privacy requests.
