Your data

Remove generated app data.

The app has no project account or video server. You control the generated files stored on your iPhone.

Clear generated exports and history

  1. Open Video Background Remover: AI.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Clear generated cache.

This removes generated previews and exports and clears their local history entries. It does not delete source videos in Photos or elsewhere.

Remove all app data

Delete the app from your iPhone to remove its local app data, subject to iOS behavior and any data copies you separately shared or retained. You can also contact oskarpolster.business@gmail.com with a privacy or data request. We do not have a server-side copy of your source videos or project history to delete.

Purchases are separate. Removing local app data or deleting the app does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's transaction records. Manage subscriptions in Apple Account settings. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase records under their own retention and privacy practices.
