Every trip, day by day

Flights, trains, stays and activities in one timeline, in the local time of each stop. Trips are shared live with everyone on them.

02

Maps that tell the story

MapKit draws flights as arcs, trains and road trips along the real routes, and every photo from the camera roll lands where and when it was taken. Three of these are my own trips.

03

An assistant that knows the trip

The assistant reads the whole itinerary. Ask what fits into three free hours and it answers from where you are and what you have not done yet; ask it to plan and it proposes a change you apply with one tap.

04

Money and the rest

Shared costs with settle-up suggestions, spending by category, a shared packing list, weather for each stop and a feed of friends' trips.
