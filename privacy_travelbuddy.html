Privacy Policy

TravelBuddy · Last updated 14 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TravelBuddy mobile application (the “App”), created by Oskar Polster (“we”, “us”, the “Service Provider”), collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is provided as a freemium service and is intended for use “as is”. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

To provide the App's features we collect the following:

  • Account information — your email address and display name, provided when you sign up with email, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address.
  • Profile content you create — username, bio, optional profile photo and banner, home city/country, preferred currency, and travel preferences.
  • Trip data you enter — trips, itineraries, flights, accommodations, activities, expenses, packing lists, notes, and any photos or documents you upload.
  • Social data — when you choose to publish a trip or follow other users, we store follow relationships, comments, and reactions so the social features work.
  • Diagnostic data — basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and crash/usage diagnostics used to keep the App stable.

The App does not collect precise background location. Map and routing features use only the place names and coordinates you enter for your trips.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and profile.
  • To store and sync your trips across your devices.
  • To power social features you opt into (sharing trips, following, comments, reactions).
  • To generate AI-assisted travel suggestions when you use the AI Assistant.
  • To process subscriptions and validate purchases.
  • To diagnose problems and improve the App.
  • To send you important service notices. We do not sell your personal data.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), file storage, and cloud functions.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in.
  • Sign in with Apple — optional sign-in.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — subscription purchases and validation.
  • Weather and currency-exchange data providers — to display forecasts and convert currencies for the destinations you enter.

We share information with these providers only to the extent necessary to operate the App. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers protected by access rules. Private content (such as your vault documents) is restricted to your account. We apply technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as you use the App. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account; this removes your profile and private data from our servers. Trips you shared with co-travelers may remain visible to them. You may also request deletion by emailing oskarpolster.business@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at oskarpolster.business@gmail.com.
