What Veil is — and what it is not

Veil displays the locations of fixed traffic-surveillance cameras, primarily automatic licence plate readers (ALPR/ANPR), sourced from OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database Licence. It then offers you a driving route that passes fewer of them.

Veil is a privacy tool, not an escape plan. It does not track police officers, patrol vehicles, checkpoints or any mobile enforcement, and it has no real-time reporting of law-enforcement activity. Obey every traffic law. Do not operate the app while driving.

The underlying data is crowd-sourced, incomplete and sometimes wrong. Treat it as a guide, never as a guarantee. A camera missing from the map is not a camera that is not there.

Attribution

Camera and street data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Some plate-reader records originate from the DeFlock mapping project. Veil is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any camera manufacturer or operator.
