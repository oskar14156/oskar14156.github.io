Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 June 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Cove app (the "Application") for mobile devices, created as a privacy-first, fully on-device AI chatbot. It is provided on an "AS IS" basis.

Core principle: on-device

Cove is a "zero-backend" application. Your chats and prompts are processed entirely on your device and never leave it. The AI models run locally on your phone or tablet. We operate no server that receives or stores your conversations.

Information collection and use

The Application does not collect your chats, prompts or generated content. They are stored only locally, in the device's private, OS-protected app storage, and are accessible only on your unlocked device. The following limited processing applies:

  • Model downloads: When you choose to download an AI model, the Application connects directly to Hugging Face's servers. Your IP address is shared with Hugging Face solely to deliver the file. This action is always initiated by you.
  • Subscriptions (Cove PRO): If you purchase a subscription, we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as a processor to manage it. RevenueCat processes an anonymous user ID, your device model, the purchase receipt and a truncated IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract); transfer to the USA relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Third-party access

Only the services above receive any data, and only as described. See their policies:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Hugging Face Privacy Policy

Tracking and advertising

Cove uses no tracking, no advertising and no analytics tools. We do not build usage profiles and do not sell data.

Your rights and choices

Because no personal chat data is stored on any server, there is nothing for us to disclose or delete on your behalf. You can delete your data from within the app at any time, or remove it irreversibly by uninstalling the Application. For data processed by RevenueCat in connection with a subscription, you may request access or deletion via the contact below.

Children

Cove is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. The AI generates content automatically; responses may be inaccurate or inappropriate.

Security

Your content stays in the device's protected app sandbox. An optional Face ID / biometric lock can further restrict access to your chats.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; changes are indicated by the effective date above.

Your consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of information as described in this Privacy Policy.
