Grundprinzip: 100 % auf dem Gerät

Cove ist eine „Zero-Backend"-App. Ihre Chats, Eingaben (Prompts) und KI-Antworten werden ausschließlich lokal auf Ihrem Gerät verarbeitet und gespeichert. Sie werden zu keinem Zeitpunkt an den Verantwortlichen oder Dritte übertragen. Es findet kein Tracking, keine Analyse (Analytics) und keine Werbung statt.

  • Chat-Inhalte: ausschließlich lokal im geschützten App-Speicher (Sandbox) Ihres Geräts; für uns nicht zugänglich.

Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (Vertragserfüllung).

Diese Anbieter verarbeiten Ihre Zahlungs- und Kontodaten als eigenständig Verantwortliche; wir erhalten keinen Zugriff auf Ihre vollständigen Zahlungsmittel. Es gelten deren Datenschutzerklärungen (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO.

Core principle: 100% on-device

Cove is a "zero-backend" app. Your chats, prompts and AI replies are processed and stored solely on your device and are never transmitted to the controller or any third party. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

What is processed

  • Chat content: only locally, in your device's protected app sandbox; not accessible to us.
  • Model downloads: downloading a model transmits your IP address to Hugging Face, Inc. (USA) (technically required); always initiated by you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.
  • Subscriptions (Cove PRO – RevenueCat): on purchase we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as a processor. It processes an anonymous user ID, device model, purchase receipt and location (country) possibly derived from your IP. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Payment processing (Apple / Google): purchases are handled exclusively via the App Store (Apple Inc., USA) and Google Play (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC, USA) as independent controllers; we never receive your full payment instrument. Their policies apply (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Transfers to the USA

Some recipients are in the USA: RevenueCat (EU Standard Contractual Clauses, Art. 46(2)(c), and where applicable Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR); Apple/Google (EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision where certified, else SCCs); Hugging Face (their policy applies). Access by US authorities cannot be excluded; check certification at dataprivacyframework.gov.

Retention

Chats/prompts: only locally until you delete them or uninstall — we never store them. Subscription/billing data: for the subscription term and then within statutory retention periods (up to 7 years) where billing-relevant. Download IP: handled by Hugging Face per their policy.

Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). Because we store no chat data on any server, you retain full control and can delete it locally anytime. No processing is based on consent, so no cookie/consent banner is required.

Right to lodge a complaint

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) — that of your residence or the controller's. The competent authority for the controller is: Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, DSB), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, www.dsb.gv.at.

Automated decision-making

No automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place. The AI replies generated locally do not produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

Children

Cove is not directed at children. In Austria the minimum age for a child's own valid consent to information-society services is 14 (§ 4(4) DSG); younger persons need a guardian's consent. We do not knowingly process data of persons under 14 without parental consent.
