Overa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Overa mobile application (the “App”), created by Oskar Polster (the “Developer”) and offered as a freemium service with optional auto-renewing subscriptions. The App is provided “as is”.

In short: Your health data — the peptides, doses, injection logs, reminders and inventory you enter — is stored only on your device. Overa has no account and runs no analytics on your data. The only information ever sent off your device is an anonymous purchase receipt, used solely to verify your subscription.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter into Overa (peptides and protocols, reconstitution calculations, dose and injection logs, vial inventory, reminders and settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to the Developer, is not stored on any server, is not synced to iCloud or Google by Overa, and is never shared with advertising or data-mining services. There is no account and no login.

Subscriptions and the only data that leaves your device

Overa offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by the platform app stores. To verify your subscription status and to provide “restore purchases”, a small amount of non-personal purchase data (such as anonymous transaction and subscription identifiers and subscription status) is processed by:

  • Apple (App Store / StoreKit) — see Apple’s Privacy Policy
  • Google (Google Play Billing) — see Google’s Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat, Inc., which validates receipts and reports subscription status on the Developer’s behalf — see RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy

Overa is configured to use an anonymous identifier for purchases. We do not ask for your name or email, we do not collect device advertising identifiers, and we do not use any advertising or tracking SDKs. Your health data is never sent to RevenueCat, Apple, Google, or any other third party for these purposes.

Apple Health (optional)

On iOS, Overa can optionally connect to Apple Health to import and export your weight and body-composition measurements (weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass). This integration is off by default and only activates after you explicitly grant permission in the system HealthKit dialog. Data read from Apple Health is stored only on your device alongside your other Overa data; data you log in Overa is written to Apple Health only while sync is enabled. Overa never transmits Apple Health data off your device, never uses it for advertising or marketing, and never shares it with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, and disable sync inside the App.

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics on your health data

Overa contains no ads, no third-party advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not run usage analytics on the health information you enter.

Data retention and deletion

Your on-device data is retained until you delete it. You can clear it at any time inside the App, or remove all of it by uninstalling the App. Purchase data processed by Apple, Google and RevenueCat is retained according to their respective policies; you may contact the Developer to request deletion of any purchase records associated with your anonymous identifier.

Children

Overa is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because your health data stays on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security. Overa additionally offers an optional Face ID / passcode app lock. Purchase data in transit is protected by the app stores’ and RevenueCat’s standard encryption.

Medical disclaimer

Overa is an informational tracking and unit-conversion tool only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or dosing recommendations. Always verify every figure independently and consult a licensed healthcare professional before making any decision.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the Developer at oskarpolster.business@gmail.com.
