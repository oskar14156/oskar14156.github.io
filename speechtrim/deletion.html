Data deletion

How to remove app-local data and request help

On your device

SpeechTrim has no account system. Delete the app from your device to remove its local settings, cached WhisperKit model and remaining app-local data. Temporary media files are cleaned up by the app after processing and can also be removed from the Files app if a copy was exported there.

Purchases

Purchase and entitlement records are handled by Apple and RevenueCat so purchases can be restored. For a request relating to purchase-support data, email oskarpolster.business@gmail.com. Do not include payment details in email.

Contact

We may need enough information to locate a support request, but SpeechTrim does not require you to create an account. We will handle deletion requests subject to records we must retain by law or for fraud prevention.
