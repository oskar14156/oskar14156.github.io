Help with silence removal, exports and Pro

SpeechTrim removes silence from audio and video and can optionally detect filler words.

Common fixes

  • If Photos import appears to wait, keep SpeechTrim open while an iCloud video downloads.
  • If filler-word detection is unavailable, verify WhisperKit model and try a supported speech language.
  • If Pro access is missing after reinstalling, open the Pro area and use Restore Purchases or Customer Center.
  • If an export fails, verify that the source file is still available and that the device has enough free storage.
