Delete your data

App: Overa

Overa has no user account and no cloud. Everything you enter (peptides, calculations, dose & injection logs, vial inventory, reminders, settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account to delete.

How to delete your data

  1. Individual entries: delete peptides, logs, vials or reminders directly in the app.
  2. All data: uninstall the app from your device — this permanently removes all locally stored data.

Purchases / subscription

Purchases are handled anonymously via the app store (Apple/Google) and RevenueCat; only an anonymous purchase receipt ever leaves your device — no health data, no name, no email. To request deletion of the purchase data stored against your anonymous identifier, email oskarpolster.business@gmail.com (processed within 30 days).
