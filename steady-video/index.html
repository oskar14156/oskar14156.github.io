Frequently Asked Questions

How does Steady stabilize videos?

Steady uses advanced optical flow and frame-by-frame camera motion estimation running entirely on your iPhone's hardware. It smooths sudden shakes and camera jitters while keeping the camera path natural.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. 100% of the video analysis, preview generation, and export rendering happen locally on your iPhone. Your personal videos never leave your device.

What is included with Steady Pro?

Steady Pro unlocks unlimited video exports at full source resolution with supported HDR color preservation.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open the Steady Pro page in the app and tap "Restore Purchases", or manage your subscription directly in your iOS App Store account settings.
