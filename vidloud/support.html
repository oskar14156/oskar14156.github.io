Quick start

  1. Tap Choose video and select a clip from Photos or Files.
  2. Pick a boost level and preview the result.
  3. Tap Export and choose where to save or share it.

Purchases

Subscriptions unlock unlimited exports. Use Restore purchases in Settings when you reinstall or move to another device with the same store account.

Test Store purchases are only available in the development build; App Store sandbox purchases are used in TestFlight.

FAQ

Does VidLoud upload my video?

No. The volume processing is performed on-device. See the Privacy Policy.

Why did an export fail?

Try a file saved locally on the device, free some storage, and export again. Very large or unsupported codecs may not be compatible.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
