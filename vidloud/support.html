Make quiet videos louder.

Choose a video or audio file, boost up to 10000%, optionally enable Reduce Noise or Voice Clarity, preview live, then export a clearer, louder copy.

Email support

Quick start

  1. Tap Choose video or Choose audio and select a clip from Photos, your gallery or Files.
  2. Pick Smart Max or a boost level up to 10000%, optionally turn on Reduce Noise or Voice Clarity, and preview live.
  3. Tap Export and choose where to save or share it.

Purchases

VidLoud Pro unlocks unlimited exports on iOS and Android. Use Restore purchases in Settings when you reinstall or move to another device with the same App Store or Google Play account.

FAQ

Does VidLoud upload my video?

No. All audio processing is performed on-device. See the Privacy Policy.

Why did an export fail?

Try a file saved locally on the device, free some storage, and export again. Very large or unsupported codecs may not be compatible.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
